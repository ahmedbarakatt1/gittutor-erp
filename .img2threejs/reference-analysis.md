# Giza accent: reference intake

Reference: Nina Aldin Thune, March 2005, photograph of the Great Pyramid (Khufu), https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kheops-Pyramid.jpg. Original URL: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/e3/Kheops-Pyramid.jpg. License selected: CC BY 2.5, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.5/. Reference downloaded on 2026-11-11. The photograph is retained only as modeling evidence, not displayed as website art. The model is a stylized independent procedural interpretation; people, ground details and sky are excluded.

1. Identification: one square-base stone pyramid dominates the image (architectural object, primaryDomain object). Confidence 0.99.
2. Silhouette: four planar triangular faces meeting at a truncated apex; the image shows two faces and a central corner. Geometric fourfold symmetry is inferred for the hidden faces. Approximate model height/base-width ratio 0.63; no survey accuracy claimed.
3. Decomposition: macro pyramid body; meso horizontal stepped masonry courses and a flat base; micro irregular joints, chipped corner blocks, pitted limestone and a slightly flattened apex. At footer scale, the masonry course rhythm is more important than individual block accuracy.
4. Relationships: courses rest flush on the immediately lower wider course; base supports all layers; apex sits flush on the final course. No detached/floating ornament.
5. Materials: limestone is opaque dielectric, nonmetallic, high roughness. Observed sunlit surface is sandy beige; shadow-side darkening is lighting, not a separate dark material.
6. Finish: matte; small albedo variations across stone. Sculpture palette will preserve beige stone but simplify variation for low display resolution. No photo projection or texture is needed because exact surface matching is outside the bounded stylized accent.
7. Identity-defining features: square footprint, converging four-face volume, 0.63 height-to-width ratio, stepped course rhythm, slightly flattened apex, exposed masonry rather than smooth casing, grounded lower edge, warm matte stone, visible central corner under oblique camera.
8. Limits: hidden rear faces inferred by symmetry; actual block pattern, entrances, human scale and exact measurements are not reconstructed. Stylized/low-poly decoration only.

Suitability: conditional pass for a bounded stylized architectural accent. Target is obvious, silhouette and material legible. The whole image includes people/sky, which are excluded from the target. Required review views: front-oblique, right, rear and left. No motion or interaction required by the website; model retains named parts for developer inspection.

Quality contract: recognizable square-based Giza pyramid at 240 by 180 CSS pixels, grounded courses without gaps, consistent slope, matte limestone, no unrelated 3D objects. A distorted silhouette, floating course, specular metal appearance or missing base contact blocks continuation. Maximum 6000 triangles and no texture downloads or animation loop.
